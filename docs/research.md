# Why Traffio

- **What it costs.** Traffio publishes its AUD tiers on its pricing page, priced on the higher of scheduled vehicles, scheduled staff or bookings. Tier 2 (up to 29 vehicles, 120 staff or 499 bookings) is A$1,860 a month; Tier 5 (up to 149 vehicles, 600 staff or 1,799 bookings) is A$5,580 a month. Every tier has every feature; the bill follows the size of the crew. https://www.traffio.com.au/pricing, checked 7 November 2026.
- **What it holds.** Bookings, people and skills, vehicles and maintenance, plans and permits, dockets signed by clients, rate schedules and invoices (Traffio help centre: Bookings, People, Plans & Permits, Vehicles, Billing, Reports).
- **Why it is data-shaped.** Under the screens are ordinary records: clients, jobs, plans, permits, shifts, crew, tickets, vehicles, dockets, incidents, invoices. The hard part is the rules (the ticket for the state, the plan, the permit's hours), and those are a database view.
- **Who pays for it.** Temporary traffic management companies in New Zealand and Australia, from 20 controllers to several hundred.
